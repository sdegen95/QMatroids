List of isomorphism classes of q-matroids on \mathbb{F}_2^4 in terms of their canonical representatives: (ID, #Bases, 0-1-Encoding)

Canonical:	The representative of each isomorphism class whose encoding is the smallest one in the whole class,
		in the sense Chapter 6, Section 4.1, of my thesis, where 0 < 1 and encodings are compared lexicographically.


0-1-Encoding: 	We totaly order the 2-spaces on \mathbb{F}_2^4 by their order of generation from the method "subspaces_fix_dim(GF(2),2,4)",
		sorted afterwards by the method "Lex_compare_subs", i.e.,
			qrank_subs = subspaces_fix_dim(GF(2),2,4); sort!(qrank_subs; lt = Lex_compare_subs).

		Then, if some 2-space is basis of a given q-matroid we assign 1 at its corresponding position and otherwise 0.
		The order and the encoding coresponds to the definitions in Chapter 6, Section 4.1, of my thesis.


Decoding: 	You can decode the 0-1-Encoding using the method "Q_Matroid_base_dec(enc_bases:: Any, kspaces::Any)", where
		"kspaces=sort!(qrank_subs; lt = Lex_compare_subs)"
