Canonical:	The representative of each isomorphism class whose encoding is the smallest one in the whole class,

0-1-Encoding: 	We totaly order the 2-spaces on \mathbb{F}_2^5 by their order of generation from the method "subspaces_fix_dim(GF(2),2,5)",
		sorted afterwards by the method "Lex_compare_subs", i.e.,
			qrank_subs = subspaces_fix_dim(GF(2),2,5); sort!(qrank_subs; lt = Lex_compare_subs).

		Then, if some 2-space is basis of a given q-matroid we assign 1 at its corresponding position and otherwise 0.
		The order and the encoding coresponds to the definitions in Chapter 6, Section 4.1, of my thesis.

		"kspaces=sort!(qrank_subs; lt = Lex_compare_subs)"
